# Wadifa Info (wadifa-info.com)

> Morocco's public-sector jobs and public-services guide: all government recruitment competitions
> (concours de la fonction publique / مباريات الوظيفة العمومية), ANAPEC private-sector offers, exam results
> and convocation lists, salary grids by grade, past exam papers, and step-by-step guides to official
> Moroccan portals (NotifRH, RSU, CNIE, passport, Cursussup, Minhaty…) and visas for Moroccans.
> Bilingual: Arabic (primary) and French. Guides are written from official sources only and name them;
> each page shows its last-update date. Updated daily.

## Open data, statistics and embeds (free to reuse with attribution)
- [Public-sector recruitment statistics 2012-2025](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/statistiques-recrutement-fonction-publique-maroc): posts announced per year, top employers and grades, application deadlines; built from the data.gov.ma open datasets "statistiques portail emploi public" (ODbL); also the 2012-2025 fragmentation series (posts per concours fell from 37.7 to 11.5; 56% of concours offer one post) and 2026 vs 2025 year-on-year
- [Entry salary by diploma](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/salaire-par-diplome-fonction-publique): net starting salary of the reference grade open to each diploma, from vocational certificate (3,985 MAD) to doctorate (13,052 MAD); study dated 2026-10-08 (Arabic: /راتب-الوظيفة-العمومية-حسب-الشهادة)
- [Public posts per 100,000 inhabitants by region](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/emploi-public-par-region): 12 regions, Aug 2024 - Oct 2026 concours vs HCP RGPH 2024 population (Laayoune-Sakia El Hamra 158, Marrakech-Safi 45); study dated 2026-10-08 (Arabic: /مناصب-الوظيفة-العمومية-حسب-الجهة)
- [Indice Wadifa du recrutement public (monthly)](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/indice-recrutement-public): concours and posts in the latest complete month vs a year earlier, top 5 employers, last 14 months; updated on the 1st of each month (Arabic: /مؤشر-التوظيف-العمومي)
- [Civil-service salary grid, 370 grades](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/grille-salariale-fonction-publique-maroc) · [AR](https://www.wadifa-info.com/سلم-رواتب-الوظيفة-العمومية): monthly net pay per grade, checked against the official MMSP simulator
- [Embeddable salary calculator](https://www.wadifa-info.com/widget/salaire) (iframe; Arabic version: /widget/salaire?lang=ar); the embed code is on the salary-grid page
- [Free JSON API and developer docs](https://www.wadifa-info.com/developpeurs): /api/v1/concours (open concours, hourly) and /api/v1/salaires (net pay by grade), no key, CORS open, CC BY 4.0 with a visible link to each item; OpenAPI at /openapi.json; iCalendar deadlines at /agenda/dates-limites; CSV files under /data/
- [Who recruits in the Moroccan civil service (study, 2024-2026)](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/qui-recrute-fonction-publique-maroc): 8,223 concours / 103,089 posts counted once; ministries 14% of concours but 66% of posts, top-10 employers 62%, health 3rd recruiter, local authorities 5.4% of posts, median application window 16 days (legal minimum 15, décret 2-11-621 art. 4), Licence the most demanded diploma, Technicien 3e grade the most frequent grade; Arabic: /من-يوظف-في-الوظيفة-العمومية
- [Moroccan public holidays and school breaks as calendar feeds](https://www.wadifa-info.com/agenda/jours-feries): iCalendar /agenda/jours-feries and /agenda/vacances-scolaires (?lang=ar), JSON /api/v1/jours-feries and /api/v1/vacances-scolaires; Islamic dates flagged approximate until confirmed
- [Gross and net pay by échelon for every civil-service grade (CSV)](https://www.wadifa-info.com/data/salaires-par-echelon-fonction-publique-maroc.csv): official MMSP simulator figures, single, no children, 25% residence zone; one row per grade and échelon; JSON twin at /data/salaires-par-echelon-fonction-publique-maroc.json
- [Teacher-training seats (CRMEF) by specialty and region](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/postes-concours-enseignement-par-specialite) · [AR](https://www.wadifa-info.com/مناصب-مباراة-التعليم-حسب-التخصص), from the official annexes, with the 2022-2025 trend
- [Annual calendar of the main concours](https://www.wadifa-info.com/fr/calendrier-concours-maroc) · [one-page PDF](https://www.wadifa-info.com/Content/docs/calendrier-concours-maroc-2026-2027.pdf)
- [Official notice PDFs hosted at stable URLs](https://www.wadifa-info.com/avis-officiel/115472) (pattern /avis-officiel/{id})
